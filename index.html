快速開始

長按預覽窗測光
上下拖曳對焦框調整 EV
雙擊預覽窗開啟 2.2× 放大鏡
對焦與測光分開。 短按重新對焦，不會把你明確設定的測光點一起搬走。

控制面板：上滑一次開啟第一層，再上滑一次開啟完整第二層；點控制列、點預覽窗或下滑可收合。大字體內容過高時，整個展開面板會作為單一連續區域捲動。

Quick Start

Tap the previewAutofocus
Long-press the previewMetering
Drag the focus frame verticallyAdjust EV
Double-tap the previewOpen the 2.2× magnifier
Focus and metering are independent. Refocusing does not move an exposure point you explicitly placed.

Control panel: swipe up once for the primary shelf and again for the full secondary shelf. Tap the bar, tap the preview, or swipe down to collapse. With large text, the expanded panel scrolls as one continuous surface when needed.

拍攝

P / S / M 與白平衡

  • P：相機協調曝光，適合快速拍攝。
  • S：以快門時間為主要控制。
  • M：手動控制曝光組合。
  • 白平衡：Auto / Lock；鎖定後可調色溫與 Tint。

照片補光

可用 Off、Auto 與 Forced。若你選擇 Forced，但目前鏡頭／系統當下無法套用強制補光，App 會停止這次拍攝並提示原因，不會靜默改成 Off。

構圖、濾鏡、Timer 與實體快門

照片支援 4:3、16:9、1:1；內建 Original、B&W、Silver。Timer 支援 3 秒／5 秒。音量鍵可作為拍照／錄影的硬體快門控制。

Shooting

P / S / M & White Balance

  • P: camera-managed exposure for fast shooting.
  • S: shutter-time-led control.
  • M: manual exposure control.
  • White Balance: Auto / Lock; when locked, Temperature and Tint are available.

Photo Flash

Off, Auto and Forced are available when supported. If Forced is selected but the current lens/system cannot apply it, the app stops that capture and explains why instead of silently switching to Off.

Composition, filters, timer & hardware shutter

Photos support 4:3, 16:9 and 1:1. Built-in looks include Original, B&W and Silver. Timers are 3s/5s. Volume buttons can act as hardware photo/video capture controls.

對焦、測光與曝光

AF / AF→MF / MF：短按預覽窗對焦；雙擊可開啟 2.2× 放大鏡，MF 時可用對焦輪微調。

EV：上下拖曳對焦框快速調整曝光補償；可用範圍依 iPhone 與拍攝狀態而異。

Spot / Zone Metering

長按預覽窗設定曝光測光位置。Auto Metering 下的長按是一次性 Spot，套用到下一張成功儲存的照片後回到 Auto。若先明確選擇 Spot 或 Zone，再長按指定位置，該模式會持續保留直到切回 Auto。Zone 會把位置吸附到九宮格最近的區域中心。

Focus Assist（Pro）

Focus Assist 分析使用者選定區域的亮度與色彩邊緣變化，提供相對清晰度提示。它不會替你控制自動對焦，也不會宣稱絕對合焦。Peak Focus 與鏡頭校正不是同一功能，只有未來 App 內正式出現時才可使用。

Focus, Metering & Exposure

AF / AF→MF / MF: tap the preview to focus; double-tap for the 2.2× magnifier, and use the focus wheel for MF fine-tuning.

EV: drag the focus frame vertically for quick exposure compensation. The available range varies with iPhone and shooting state.

Spot / Zone Metering

Long-press the preview to place the exposure-metering point. From Auto Metering, the long press creates a one-shot Spot for the next successfully saved photo and then returns to Auto. If Spot or Zone is explicitly selected first, that mode persists until you return to Auto. Zone snaps the point to the nearest center of the 3×3 grid.

Focus Assist (Pro)

Focus Assist analyzes brightness and color-edge changes in a user-selected region to provide relative sharpness guidance. It does not control autofocus or claim absolute focus. Peak Focus and lens calibration are separate future features and are available only if they appear in the app.

格式與影片

HEIFBasic 標準照片格式
JPEG / PNGPro 相容／無損輸出
Bayer RAWPro+ · DNG
Apple ProRAWPro+ · DNG
RAW 只儲存 DNG。 不會另外建立 JPEG／HEIF 副本，也不會在 RAW 失敗時偷偷改存一般格式。
RAW 裝置能力與溫度

RAW／ProRAW 只會在目前 iPhone 與鏡頭支援時顯示。RAW 處理量較高，長時間連續拍攝可能使裝置升溫；iOS 可能在高溫時限制部分能力。

影片

Basic 支援 1080p；Pro 可使用 iPhone 實際支援的 4K 模式，包括支援時的 4K60。Video Auto torch 只在錄影開始後依亮度條件調整。自訂比例或濾鏡處理若失敗，App 不會把不符合設定的影片假裝成成功結果。

Formats & Video

HEIFBasic standard photo format
JPEG / PNGPro compatibility / lossless output
Bayer RAWPro+ · DNG
Apple ProRAWPro+ · DNG
RAW is DNG-only. No JPEG/HEIF companion is created, and a failed RAW capture is not silently replaced with an ordinary format.
RAW device support & thermals

RAW/ProRAW appears only when the current iPhone and lens support it. RAW processing is heavier and continuous use may warm the device; iOS can temporarily limit capabilities at high temperatures.

Video

Basic supports 1080p. Pro can use 4K modes actually supported by the iPhone, including 4K60 when available. Video Auto torch operates only after recording begins. If custom-ratio or filter processing fails, the app does not present a mismatched video as a successful result.

工具與方案

Memory

保存五項照片設定：EV、對焦模式、白平衡、比例、濾鏡。不保存焦距、對焦／測光位置、數位變焦、鏡頭、快門或 ISO。

Memory 快速重設

目前設定仍與 Memory 完全相同時，再點一次該 Memory 會退出套用狀態，並將五項受管理設定重設為 EV 0.0、AF、Auto WB、4:3、Original；已保存的 Memory 不會被覆寫。

浮動工具列與外接鏡

浮球可自訂常用操作、記住位置並在閒置後收合。外接鏡 Profile 可保存並管理鏡頭相關設定；實際可用項目依 iPhone 與鏡頭而異。

Basic / Pro / Pro+

  • Basic：手動拍攝基礎與 HEIF。
  • Pro：Focus Assist、JPEG／PNG、支援裝置上的 4K 模式等。
  • Pro+：支援裝置／鏡頭上的 Bayer RAW、Apple ProRAW → DNG。

Tools & Tiers

Memory

Stores five photo settings: EV, focus mode, white balance, aspect ratio and filter. It does not store focus distance, focus/metering point, digital zoom, lens, shutter or ISO.

Memory quick reset

While the current settings still exactly match a Memory, tapping it again exits the applied state and resets the five managed values to EV 0.0, AF, Auto WB, 4:3 and Original. The saved Memory itself is not overwritten.

Floating Toolbar & External Lenses

The floating tool can be customized, remembers its position and collapses after inactivity. External-lens Profiles can store and manage lens-related settings; available options vary with the iPhone and lens.

Basic / Pro / Pro+

  • Basic: manual-shooting essentials and HEIF.
  • Pro: Focus Assist, JPEG/PNG, supported 4K modes and more.
  • Pro+: Bayer RAW and Apple ProRAW → DNG on supported iPhone/lens paths.

圖庫

App 內 Gallery 用來檢視你授權的 Apple Photos 內容：照片可換頁、縮放與拖曳；影片可播放／暫停與拖曳進度。

管理或刪除媒體請使用 Apple「照片」App。 Honesmith Optics 目前不在 App 內直接執行刪除。

支援 Photos Full Access 與 Limited Access。iCloud Photos 等 Apple 服務仍由 Apple 與你的系統設定管理。

Gallery

The in-app Gallery is for viewing authorized Apple Photos content: page through photos, zoom and pan; play/pause videos and scrub playback.

Manage or delete media in Apple Photos. Honesmith Optics does not currently delete saved media inside the app.

Photos Full Access and Limited Access are supported. Apple services such as iCloud Photos remain controlled by Apple and your system settings.

診斷、隱私與說明

診斷

診斷只在你主動建立時產生，不會自動上傳。報告可能包含 App 版本、拍攝設定摘要、Focus Assist 狀態、近期測光／RAW 儲存結果、最近補光請求的 requested / supported / applied 結果與裝置溫度；不包含照片、影片、RAW 內容、位置、裝置識別碼、網路名稱、完整檔案路徑或檔名。

隱私

  • 無 Honesmith 帳號。
  • 無廣告、無使用行為分析。
  • 照片、影片、RAW 與診斷不會自動上傳。
權限與網路

Camera 用於取景／拍攝；Microphone 只在有聲錄影時使用；Photos 用於保存 App 產生的媒體與顯示你授權 Gallery 存取的內容。購買／恢復購買與說明更新需要網路。

完整說明如何離線更新？

開啟資訊頁時，如果有網路，App 會嘗試下載最新版說明並保留本機副本。沒有網路時使用最近一次成功更新的版本；若從未更新成功，才使用 App 內建版本。更新請求不會附帶照片、影片、RAW 或診斷內容。

常見問題
  • 看不到 RAW / ProRAW？ 先確認 Pro+，再確認目前 iPhone／鏡頭是否支援且 App 有顯示選項。
  • RAW 沒有 JPEG／HEIF？ 正常；RAW 只存 DNG。
  • 長按後出現 AE？ 長按是測光，短按仍是對焦。
  • Forced Flash 無法拍？ 代表當下 camera path 無法套用強制補光；App 會明確提示，不會靜默改成 Off。

Diagnostics, Privacy & Help

Diagnostics

Diagnostics are created only when you request them and are not uploaded automatically. A report may include app version, shooting-setting summaries, Focus Assist state, recent metering/RAW-save results, recent flash requested/supported/applied outcomes and device thermal state. It does not contain photo/video/RAW content, location, device identifiers, network names, full file paths or filenames.

Privacy

  • No Honesmith account.
  • No ads or usage analytics.
  • No automatic upload of photos, videos, RAW media or diagnostics.
Permissions & network

Camera is used for preview/capture; Microphone only for video with audio; Photos for saving app-created media and displaying Gallery content you authorize. Purchases/restores and guide updates require a network connection.

How does offline help update?

When you open Info with a connection, the app tries to download the latest guide and keeps a local copy. Offline, it uses the most recently updated copy; if no update has ever succeeded, it falls back to the bundled guide. The update request does not attach photos, videos, RAW media or diagnostics.

Troubleshooting
  • No RAW / ProRAW option? Confirm Pro+ and check whether the current iPhone/lens path supports and displays it.
  • No JPEG/HEIF next to RAW? Expected; RAW is DNG-only.
  • AE marker after a long press? Long press meters; tap still focuses.
  • Forced Flash capture blocked? The current camera path could not apply forced flash; the app reports this instead of silently switching to Off.
