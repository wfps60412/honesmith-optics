相機與手動控制

拍攝模式

Honesmith Optics 保留 P／S／M 等手動拍攝控制。可用項目會依目前鏡頭、拍攝格式與裝置能力調整。

  • P：由相機協調曝光參數，適合快速拍攝。
  • S：以快門時間為主要控制項，搭配可用的 ISO／曝光控制。
  • M：以手動方式控制曝光組合。
  • 白平衡：可使用 Auto／Lock 與現有手動白平衡控制。
  • 實體鏡頭：鏡頭選擇會依你的 iPhone 實際提供的鏡頭顯示；重新點選目前鏡頭可回到 1.0× 數位變焦。

構圖比例

照片支援 4:3、16:9、1:1。取景畫面以實際構圖為準，不以拉伸影像假造比例。

濾鏡

內建 Original、B&W、Silver。濾鏡是對已拍攝訊號的色調處理，不生成不存在的影像細節。

頂部快捷與浮動控制

比例、濾鏡、測光等常用項目可從相機介面的快捷控制存取。工具列使用指定滑動手勢展開／收合，避免普通點按誤觸。浮動控制會記住位置並在閒置後收合。

Timer 與省電

支援 3 秒／5 秒計時拍攝。Power Saving 會降低 App 顯示亮度而不是把畫面蓋黑；錄影時系統自動鎖定會依既有安全規則處理。

Camera & Manual Controls

Shooting modes

Honesmith Optics provides P/S/M-style manual camera control. Available settings vary with the active lens, capture format, and your iPhone.

  • P:Camera-managed exposure behavior for fast shooting.
  • S:Shutter-time-led control with the available ISO/exposure controls.
  • M:Manual exposure control.
  • White balance:Auto/Lock and the app's existing manual white-balance controls.
  • Physical lenses:Lens choices match the cameras available on your iPhone. Re-selecting the current lens returns digital zoom to 1.0×.

Aspect ratios

Photos support 4:3, 16:9, and 1:1. The live view reflects the intended composition without stretching the image.

Filters

Built-in looks include Original, B&W, and Silver. They adjust the look of the photo without inventing image detail that was not captured.

Top shortcuts & floating control

Frequently used controls such as ratio, filter, and metering are available from the camera shortcuts. The toolbar uses its designated swipe gesture to expand/collapse, reducing accidental activation. The floating control remembers its position and collapses after inactivity.

Timer & Power Saving

3-second and 5-second timers are available. Power Saving reduces app display brightness instead of placing a black overlay over the live view. Recording uses the app's existing idle-lock safety behavior.

對焦、測光與曝光

AF / MF

相機支援既有 AF、AF→MF 與 MF 工作方式。短按取景畫面設定對焦；需要精細確認時可雙擊開啟 2.2× 放大鏡，再用右側對焦輪微調。

獨立 Spot Metering

長按取景畫面會設定曝光測光點。這個點與對焦點分離，因此可「對焦在 A、測光在 B」。AE 標記用來表示目前測光位置。

操作方式：
短按＝Focus · 長按＝Exposure Metering · 對焦標記上下拖曳＝EV · 雙擊＝Magnifier

若你原本在 Auto Metering，明確長按放置測光點時會進入 Spot Metering。重新短按對焦不會偷偷覆寫這個明確設定的曝光點。

記住：短按控制對焦位置，長按控制曝光測光位置。

Zone Metering

使用分區測光時，長按預覽後，測光點會吸附至九宮格中最接近的區域中心。這是測光位置的吸附規則，不代表 App 對整個區域做自行推測的平均曝光。

EV

在支援的狀態下，垂直拖曳對焦標記可快速做曝光補償。實際可調範圍會依你的 iPhone 與目前拍攝狀態而不同。

Focus, Metering & Exposure

AF / MF

The camera supports AF, AF→MF, and MF. Tap the viewfinder to focus. For precise focus, double-tap to open the 2.2× loupe and fine-tune with the right-side focus wheel.

Independent Spot Metering

Long-press the viewfinder to place the exposure-metering point. It is independent from the focus point, so you can focus on one subject while metering another area. The AE marker indicates the active metering position.

Controls:
Tap = Focus · Long press = Exposure Metering · Vertical focus-reticle drag = EV · Double tap = Magnifier

If Auto Metering is active, explicitly placing a point by long press moves the metering behavior to Spot. A later tap-to-focus does not silently overwrite that explicit exposure point.

Remember: a short tap controls focus, while a long press controls exposure metering.

Zone Metering

With Zone Metering, a long press snaps the metering point to the nearest center of the 3×3 grid. This is a metering-position snap rule, not a claim that the app invents or estimates exposure from an entire zone.

EV

When supported by the active state, drag the focus reticle vertically for quick exposure compensation. The available range may vary with your iPhone and current shooting setup.

Focus Assist

Focus Assist 是 Pro 的取景分析工具，用來協助判讀目前選定區域的清晰度。它不會替你自動控制鏡頭對焦，也不會生成不存在的影像細節。

  • 使用單一使用者選定區域進行取景分析。
  • 利用亮度與色彩邊緣的變化，協助判斷清晰度。
  • 可與放大鏡、手動對焦輪一起使用。
  • 使用者關閉後，後續啟動／恢復購買不應強制重新開啟。
Peak Focus 不等於 Focus Assist。Peak Focus 與鏡頭校正屬後續擴充，不能因為看見 Focus Assist 就假設這些功能已存在。

Focus Assist

Focus Assist is a Pro viewfinder-analysis tool that helps you judge sharpness in the selected region. It does not take control of autofocus and does not generate image detail that was never captured.

  • Analyzes a single user-selected viewfinder region.
  • Uses brightness and color-edge changes to help you judge sharpness.
  • Works alongside the loupe and manual focus wheel.
  • If you turn it off, the app remembers your choice the next time you use it.
Peak Focus is not the same as Focus Assist.Peak Focus and lens calibration remain later expansions and must not be inferred from the presence of Focus Assist.

RAW / DNG（Pro+）

使用前確認：只有當 App 內出現 RAW／ProRAW 選項，且你的 iPhone 與目前鏡頭支援時，才能使用這些格式。

在支援的 iPhone 與鏡頭上，Pro+ 提供兩種 RAW 格式：

Bayer RAW儲存為 DNG。
Apple ProRAW儲存為 DNG。

RAW 儲存方式

RAW 模式只儲存 DNG。RAW 照片會只儲存為 DNG，不會另外產生一張 JPEG 或 HEIF 副本。

RAW 不會自動改存成其他格式。
如果 RAW 無法完成，App 會直接提示不支援或拍攝失敗，不會改存成 JPEG／HEIF 來取代 RAW。

裝置能力

RAW／ProRAW 是否可用會依你的 iPhone、目前鏡頭與 iOS 版本而不同。App 只會顯示目前可以使用的選項。

熱與連續拍攝

RAW 比一般照片需要更多處理，連續拍攝時 iPhone 可能較容易發熱。溫度過高時，系統可能暫時限制拍攝。

RAW / DNG (Pro+)

Before you shoot: RAW/ProRAW is available only when the option appears in the app and your current iPhone and lens support it.

On supported iPhones and lenses, Pro+ provides two RAW formats:

Bayer RAWSaved as DNG.
Apple ProRAWSaved as DNG.

How RAW is saved

RAW photos are saved as DNG only. The app does not create an additional JPEG or HEIF copy.

RAW is not automatically replaced by another format.
If RAW cannot be completed, the app reports that it is unsupported or that capture failed instead of saving JPEG/HEIF as a substitute.

Device support

RAW/ProRAW availability varies by iPhone, current lens, and iOS version. The app shows only the options available to you.

Thermals & continuous use

RAW requires more processing than ordinary photos, so continuous shooting may warm the iPhone more quickly. If the device becomes too hot, iOS may temporarily limit capture.

照片格式與輸出

  • Basic：HEIF。
  • Pro：HEIF、JPEG 相容性輸出、PNG App 處理後無損編碼。
  • Pro+：在支援的裝置／鏡頭上提供 Bayer RAW／Apple ProRAW → DNG。

JPEG 是相容性格式，不代表畫質一定高於 HEIF。PNG 是 App 處理後照片的無損格式，不是 RAW。

一般照片若套用比例或濾鏡，App 會依你選擇的設定完成輸出；如果處理失敗，會明確提示，而不會假裝成功。

Photo Formats & Output

  • Basic: HEIF.
  • Pro: HEIF, JPEG compatibility output, and PNG lossless encoding of app-processed output.
  • Pro+: Bayer RAW / Apple ProRAW → DNG on supported device/lens paths.

JPEG is provided for compatibility; it is not a claim of inherently higher quality than HEIF. PNG is a lossless format for photos processed by the app; it is not RAW.

For ordinary photos using an aspect ratio or filter, the app uses your selected settings. If processing fails, the app tells you instead of pretending the result succeeded.

影片

  • Basic 支援 1080p 錄影；Pro 可使用 iPhone 實際支援的 4K 模式，包括支援時的 4K60。
  • 音量鍵可作為錄影開始／停止控制；在圖庫、購買畫面、系統中斷或影片仍在儲存時不會觸發錄影。
  • Video Auto torch 只在實際錄影開始後運作；會依亮度條件自適應開／關，不在待機時持續點亮。
  • 16:9 Original 直接使用 iPhone 的一般錄影方式；自訂比例或濾鏡由 App 套用所選效果，並維持正確方向與聲音同步。
  • 若處理失敗，不應把不符合所選比例／濾鏡的影片默默當成成功結果。

Video

  • Basic supports 1080p recording. Pro can use the 4K modes your iPhone supports, including 4K60 when available.
  • Volume buttons can start or stop recording. They do not trigger recording while you are in Gallery, a purchase screen, a system interruption, or while the previous video is still being saved.
  • Video Auto torch runs only after recording begins. It can switch on/off adaptively with scene brightness instead of remaining active while idle.
  • 16:9 Original uses the iPhone’s normal recording output. Custom ratios or filters are applied by the app while keeping orientation and audio in sync.
  • If processing fails, the app must not silently present a video that does not match the selected ratio/filter as a success.

浮動工具列

浮動工具列是可自訂的快速操作入口，和 iOS 系統 AssistiveTouch 無關。

  • 可在設定中啟用／停用。
  • 可選擇要放入的動作；至少保留一個動作。
  • 可用動作依目前 App 版本與裝置能力而定，例如快門、濾鏡、測光、Timer、暗簾／補光相關入口。
  • 拖曳後會記住最後位置。
  • 操作時會恢復清楚顯示；閒置約 1.5 秒後自動收合，並回到你設定的待機透明度。
  • 展開時會往畫面內側排列，避免按鈕直接蓋住浮動球。

Floating Toolbar

The floating toolbar is a customizable quick-action surface. It is separate from iOS AssistiveTouch.

  • Enable or disable it in settings.
  • Choose which actions are present; at least one action remains enabled.
  • Available actions depend on the features shown on your iPhone and can include capture, filter, metering, timer, and light-related shortcuts.
  • The last dragged position is remembered.
  • It becomes fully visible while you use it. After about 1.5 seconds of inactivity, it collapses and returns to your chosen idle transparency.
  • Expanded actions are placed inward from the screen edge so they do not sit directly on top of the floating ball.

Memory（拍攝記憶）

Memory 只保存一組明確限定的照片設定，不是完整相機狀態快照。

會保存EV、對焦控制模式（AF／AF→MF／MF）、白平衡、照片比例、照片濾鏡。
不會保存對焦距離、對焦點、數位變焦、測光點、鏡頭選擇、快門、ISO、影片設定。

套用與快速重設

  • 套用 Memory 後，五項受管理設定會套用到目前拍攝狀態。
  • 若修改其中任一項，該 Memory 不再視為目前完全吻合。
  • 當目前設定仍與 Memory 相同時，再次點選該 Memory，會退出目前套用狀態並把這五項快速重設為：EV 0.0、AF（持續自動對焦）、Auto WB、4:3、Original。
  • 快速重設不會改寫已儲存的 Memory；之後仍可再次套用原本保存的設定。

Memory

Memory stores a deliberately limited photo setup. It is not a snapshot of the entire camera state.

StoredEV, focus-control mode (AF / AF→MF / MF), white balance, photo aspect ratio, and photo filter.
Not storedFocus distance, focus point, digital zoom, metering point, lens choice, shutter, ISO, or video settings.

Apply & reset

  • Applying a Memory applies the five managed values to the current photo setup.
  • Changing any one of those five values means the active setup no longer fully matches that Memory.
  • While the current settings still match, tapping that Memory again exits it and resets the five values to EV 0.0, continuous AF, Auto WB, 4:3, and Original.
  • Resetting does not overwrite the saved Memory; you can apply the saved setup again later.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics help describe app state and problems. They are not a background tracking service.

  • Diagnostic reports are created locally and are not uploaded automatically.
  • They leave the device only after you explicitly generate/export them through an iOS destination you choose.
  • Diagnostic reports do not include photo/video content, RAW media, metering positions, location, contacts, payment data, device identifiers, network names, full file paths, or filenames.
  • Diagnostic reports may include the app version, camera type, settings summary, Focus Assist status, recent metering/RAW save results, device temperature state, and a random report number.
When reporting an issue, use the diagnostic report exported by the app rather than sharing full system logs, photos, or other material that may contain personal information.

  • 不設 Honesmith 帳號系統。

  • 不自動上傳你的照片、影片、RAW 或診斷。
  • 診斷只有在你主動產生／匯出時才會透過你選擇的 iOS 分享流程離開裝置。

  • Microphone：只有錄製含聲音的影片時使用。

購買／恢復購買與完整說明更新需要網路。當你開啟使用說明時，App 會嘗試下載最新版並保存一份在本機，供之後離線閱讀。這個更新不會包含你的照片、影片或診斷。

Privacy, Permissions & Network

Local operation

Core camera, Gallery, editing, settings, and diagnostics are designed to operate locally, without a Honesmith account or Honesmith cloud photo-processing service.

  • No Honesmith account system.
  • No ads or usage analytics.
  • No automatic upload of your photos, videos, RAW media, or diagnostics.
  • Diagnostics leave the device only when you explicitly export/share them through an iOS destination you choose.

System permissions

  • Camera:Live preview and capture.
  • Microphone:Used only when recording video with audio.
  • Photos:Save app-created media and display photos/videos you authorize the in-app Gallery to access.

When is a network connection needed?

Purchases/restores and guide updates require a connection. When you open Help, the app tries to download the latest guide and keeps a local copy for offline reading. Your photos, videos, and diagnostics are not included in that update.

常見問題

為什麼我看不到 RAW 或 ProRAW？

先確認你已購買 Pro+，再確認 App 內是否顯示 RAW／ProRAW 選項。不同 iPhone 與鏡頭支援的 RAW 格式可能不同。

為什麼 RAW 沒有附一張 JPEG／HEIF？

這是正常的。RAW 照片只儲存 DNG，不會另外產生 JPEG／HEIF 副本。

長按畫面後為什麼出現 AE 標記？

長按是獨立曝光測光操作。短按仍是對焦；兩者故意分離。

購買 Pro/Pro+ 後，為什麼某些選項仍不可用？

購買 Pro／Pro+ 只會解鎖 App 提供的功能；iPhone 或目前鏡頭本身不支援的格式與功能仍不會出現。

為什麼線上說明打不開？

第一次開啟使用說明時若有網路，App 會自動保存最新版。之後即使沒有網路，也會顯示最近一次成功更新的版本；如果從未成功更新過，則使用 App 內建說明。

Troubleshooting

Why don't I see RAW or ProRAW?

First make sure Pro+ is purchased, then check whether RAW/ProRAW appears in the app. Supported RAW formats vary by iPhone and lens.

Why is there no JPEG/HEIF next to my RAW file?

This is normal. RAW photos are saved as DNG only, without an additional JPEG/HEIF copy.

Why does an AE marker appear after a long press?

Long press is the independent exposure-metering action. A normal tap still focuses; the two are intentionally separate.

Why is a feature unavailable even after purchasing Pro/Pro+?

Purchasing Pro/Pro+ unlocks features the app provides, but formats or features unsupported by your iPhone or current lens will remain unavailable.

Why won't the online guide load?

When you open Help with a connection, the app automatically saves the latest guide. Later, it shows the most recently updated copy even when offline. If no update has ever succeeded, the bundled guide is used.
