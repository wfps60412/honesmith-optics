相機與手動控制

拍攝模式

Honesmith Optics 保留 P／S／M 等手動拍攝控制。可用項目會依目前鏡頭、拍攝格式與裝置能力調整。

  • P：由相機協調曝光參數，適合快速拍攝。
  • S：以快門時間為主要控制項，搭配可用的 ISO／曝光控制。
  • M：以手動方式控制曝光組合。
  • 白平衡：可使用 Auto／Lock 與現有手動白平衡控制。
  • 實體鏡頭：鏡頭選擇依裝置實際提供的 camera capability；重新點選目前鏡頭可依既有操作回到 1.0× digital zoom。

構圖比例

照片支援 4:3、16:9、1:1。取景畫面以實際構圖為準，不以拉伸影像假造比例。

濾鏡

內建 Original、B&W、Silver。濾鏡是對已拍攝訊號的色調處理，不生成不存在的影像細節。

頂部快捷與浮動控制

比例、濾鏡、測光等常用項目可從相機介面的快捷控制存取。工具列使用指定滑動手勢展開／收合，避免普通點按誤觸。浮動控制會記住位置並在閒置後收合。

Timer 與省電

支援 3 秒／5 秒計時拍攝。Power Saving 會降低 App 顯示亮度而不是把畫面蓋黑；錄影時系統自動鎖定會依既有安全規則處理。

Camera & Manual Controls

Shooting modes

Honesmith Optics provides P/S/M-style manual camera control. Available settings vary with the active lens, capture format, and device capability.

  • P:Camera-managed exposure behavior for fast shooting.
  • S:Shutter-time-led control with the available ISO/exposure controls.
  • M:Manual exposure control.
  • White balance:Auto/Lock and the app's existing manual white-balance controls.
  • Physical lenses:Lens choices come from runtime camera capability. Re-selecting the active physical lens follows the existing 1.0× digital-zoom reset behavior.

Aspect ratios

Photos support 4:3, 16:9, and 1:1. The live view reflects the intended composition without stretching the image.

Filters

Built-in looks include Original, B&W, and Silver. They are deterministic tonal treatments of captured image data; they do not generate missing image detail.

Top shortcuts & floating control

Frequently used controls such as ratio, filter, and metering are available from the camera shortcuts. The toolbar uses its designated swipe gesture to expand/collapse, reducing accidental activation. The floating control remembers its position and collapses after inactivity.

Timer & Power Saving

3-second and 5-second timers are available. Power Saving reduces app display brightness instead of placing a black overlay over the live view. Recording uses the app's existing idle-lock safety behavior.

對焦、測光與曝光

AF / MF

相機支援既有 AF、AF→MF 與 MF 工作方式。短按取景畫面設定對焦；需要精細確認時可雙擊開啟 2.2× 放大鏡，再用右側對焦輪微調。

獨立 Spot Metering

長按取景畫面會設定曝光測光點。這個點與對焦點分離，因此可「對焦在 A、測光在 B」。AE 標記用來表示目前測光位置。

Build 53 起的操作：
短按＝Focus · 長按＝Exposure Metering · 對焦標記上下拖曳＝EV · 雙擊＝Magnifier

若你原本在 Auto Metering，明確長按放置測光點時會進入 Spot Metering。重新短按對焦不會偷偷覆寫這個明確設定的曝光點。

EV

在支援的狀態下，垂直拖曳對焦標記可快速做曝光補償。實際可調範圍由裝置與目前 session capability 決定。

Focus, Metering & Exposure

AF / MF

The camera keeps its AF, AF→MF, and MF workflows. Tap the viewfinder to focus. For critical focus, double-tap to open the 2.2× loupe and fine-tune with the right-side focus wheel.

Independent Spot Metering

Long-press the viewfinder to place the exposure-metering point. It is independent from the focus point, so you can focus on one subject while metering another area. The AE marker indicates the active metering position.

Build 53+ interaction:
Tap = Focus · Long press = Exposure Metering · Vertical focus-reticle drag = EV · Double tap = Magnifier

If Auto Metering is active, explicitly placing a point by long press moves the metering behavior to Spot. A later tap-to-focus does not silently overwrite that explicit exposure point.

EV

When supported by the active state, drag the focus reticle vertically for quick exposure compensation. The actual range depends on device and current session capability.

Focus Assist

Focus Assist 是 Pro 的取景分析工具，用來協助判讀目前選定區域的清晰度。它不會替你自動控制鏡頭對焦，也不會生成不存在的影像細節。

  • 使用單一使用者選定區域進行取景分析。
  • 搭配亮度與原生色彩邊緣證據，提供清晰度輔助。
  • 可與放大鏡、手動對焦輪一起使用。
  • 使用者關閉後，後續啟動／恢復購買不應強制重新開啟。
Peak Focus 不等於 Focus Assist。Peak Focus 與鏡頭校正屬後續擴充，不能因為看見 Focus Assist 就假設這些功能已存在。

Focus Assist

Focus Assist is a Pro viewfinder-analysis tool that helps you judge sharpness in the selected region. It does not take control of autofocus and does not generate image detail that was never captured.

  • Analyzes a single user-selected viewfinder region.
  • Uses luminance and native color-edge evidence to assist focus judgment.
  • Works alongside the loupe and manual focus wheel.
  • Once you explicitly turn it off, later launches or entitlement refreshes should respect that preference.
Peak Focus is not the same as Focus Assist.Peak Focus and lens calibration remain later expansions and must not be inferred from the presence of Focus Assist.

RAW / DNG（Pro+）

適用範圍：只有當你安裝的 App 版本實際提供 Pro+ RAW、且 App 顯示有效權益與 runtime capability 時，本節才代表可操作功能。若 App 內沒有 RAW／ProRAW 入口，請視為該版本目前不可用。

在裝置與目前相機路徑支援時，Pro+ RAW 契約包含兩種 RAW 家族：

Bayer RAW儲存為 DNG。
Apple ProRAW儲存為 DNG。

DNG-only 工作方式

RAW 拍攝是一條獨立的拍攝路徑。選擇 RAW 後，成功結果是 DNG；正式流程不另外建立 HEIF／JPEG 快速副本。

RAW 不會偷偷降級。
如果要求的 RAW 無法完成，App 應回報不支援或失敗；不會把 processed HEIF／JPEG 當成 RAW 成功結果。

裝置能力

RAW／ProRAW 是否可用依目前裝置、實體鏡頭、iOS 與 runtime camera capability 判定，不以「某一款 iPhone 一定支援」的方式保證。

熱與連續拍攝

RAW 處理可能比一般照片帶來更高的運算與熱負載。裝置在高熱狀態下可能限制或拒絕部分工作；這不會改變 DNG-only 的輸出契約。

RAW / DNG (Pro+)

Scope: This section describes an operable feature only when your installed app build actually includes Pro+ RAW and the app presents a valid entitlement plus runtime capability. If RAW/ProRAW is not shown in the app, treat it as unavailable in that build.

When supported by the device and active camera path, the Pro+ RAW contract includes two RAW families:

Bayer RAWSaved as DNG.
Apple ProRAWSaved as DNG.

DNG-only workflow

RAW capture is a separate capture path. When RAW is selected, a successful result is a DNG. The shipping workflow does not create an additional HEIF/JPEG quick companion.

RAW does not silently downgrade.
If the requested RAW capture cannot complete, the app reports unsupported/failure rather than presenting a processed HEIF/JPEG as a successful RAW result.

Device capability

RAW/ProRAW availability is determined by the current device, physical lens, iOS version, and runtime camera capability. The app does not promise that every iPhone supports every RAW mode.

Thermals & continuous use

RAW may create a higher compute and thermal load than ordinary processed photos. Under high thermal pressure, the device may limit or refuse work; this does not change the DNG-only output contract.

照片格式與輸出

  • Basic：HEIF。
  • Pro：HEIF、JPEG 相容性輸出、PNG App 處理後無損編碼。
  • Pro+：在支援的裝置／鏡頭上提供 Bayer RAW／Apple ProRAW → DNG。

JPEG 是相容性格式，不代表畫質一定高於 HEIF。PNG 是 App 處理結果的無損編碼，不是 sensor RAW。

一般 processed photo 若套用比例或濾鏡，App 會盡量完成所選結果；無法完成時必須以可見方式處理失敗／退化，而不是假裝成功。

Photo Formats & Output

  • Basic: HEIF.
  • Pro: HEIF, JPEG compatibility output, and PNG lossless encoding of app-processed output.
  • Pro+: Bayer RAW / Apple ProRAW → DNG on supported device/lens paths.

JPEG is provided for compatibility; it is not a claim of inherently higher quality than HEIF. PNG is lossless encoding of app-processed output, not sensor RAW.

For ordinary processed photos using an aspect ratio or filter, the app attempts to produce the selected result. If it cannot, failure/degradation must be surfaced rather than presented as a false success.

影片

  • Basic 具備既有 1080p 錄影基線；Pro 可使用裝置實際支援的 4K 模式，包括支援時的 4K60。
  • 音量鍵可作為錄影開始／停止控制，但在 Gallery、購買介面、系統中斷或 finalizing 等狀態會被安全阻擋。
  • Video Auto torch 只在實際錄影開始後運作；會依亮度條件自適應開／關，不在待機時持續點亮。
  • 16:9 Original 使用原生錄影路徑；自訂比例或濾鏡影片走 App 處理路徑並維持方向／音訊同步。
  • 若處理失敗，不應把不符合所選比例／濾鏡的影片默默當成成功結果。

Video

  • Basic keeps the existing 1080p recording baseline. Pro can use actual supported 4K modes, including 4K60 when the device supports it.
  • Volume buttons can start/stop recording, but capture input is safely blocked while Gallery, purchase UI, interruption, finalization, or similar blocking states are active.
  • Video Auto torch runs only after recording begins. It can switch on/off adaptively with scene brightness instead of remaining active while idle.
  • 16:9 Original uses the native recording path. Custom ratios or filters use the app processing path while preserving orientation and audio timing.
  • If processing fails, the app must not silently present a video that does not match the selected ratio/filter as a success.

浮動工具列

浮動工具列是可自訂的快速操作入口，和 iOS 系統 AssistiveTouch 無關。

  • 可在設定中啟用／停用。
  • 可選擇要放入的動作；至少保留一個動作。
  • 可用動作依目前 App 版本與裝置能力而定，例如快門、濾鏡、測光、Timer、暗簾／補光相關入口。
  • 拖曳後會記住最後位置。
  • 互動時回到完整不透明度；閒置約 1.5 秒後自動收合並回到設定的 idle opacity。
  • 展開時會往畫面內側排列，避免按鈕直接蓋住浮動球。

Floating Toolbar

The floating toolbar is a customizable quick-action surface. It is separate from iOS AssistiveTouch.

  • Enable or disable it in settings.
  • Choose which actions are present; at least one action remains enabled.
  • Available actions depend on the installed app version and device capability, and can include capture, filter, metering, timer, dark-curtain/light-related actions.
  • The last dragged position is remembered.
  • Interaction returns it to full opacity; after about 1.5 seconds of inactivity it collapses and returns to the configured idle opacity.
  • Expanded actions are placed inward from the screen edge so they do not sit directly on top of the floating ball.

Memory（拍攝記憶）

Memory 只保存一組明確限定的照片設定，不是完整相機狀態快照。

會保存EV、對焦控制模式（AF／AF→MF／MF）、白平衡、照片比例、照片濾鏡。
不會保存對焦距離、對焦點、數位變焦、測光點、鏡頭選擇、快門、ISO、影片設定。

套用與 quick reset

  • 套用 Memory 後，五項受管理設定會套用到目前拍攝狀態。
  • 若修改其中任一項，該 Memory 不再視為目前完全吻合。
  • 在仍吻合時再次點選 active Memory，會退出 active 狀態並快速重設這五項：EV 0.0、continuous AF、Auto WB、4:3、Original。
  • Quick reset 不會改寫已儲存的 Memory 本身；之後仍可重新套用原本保存的內容。

Memory

Memory stores a deliberately limited photo setup. It is not a snapshot of the entire camera state.

StoredEV, focus-control mode (AF / AF→MF / MF), white balance, photo aspect ratio, and photo filter.
Not storedFocus distance, focus point, digital zoom, metering point, lens choice, shutter, ISO, or video settings.

Apply & quick reset

  • Applying a Memory applies the five managed values to the current photo setup.
  • Changing any one of those five values means the active setup no longer fully matches that Memory.
  • While it still matches, tapping the active Memory again exits the active state and quickly resets those five values to EV 0.0, continuous AF, Auto WB, 4:3, and Original.
  • Quick reset does not overwrite the saved Memory itself; the saved setup can still be applied again later.

  • 照片：fit、縮放、雙擊 zoom、放大後平移；fit 狀態可左右換頁。
  • 影片：播放／暫停、進度拖曳、離開頁面時停止播放。
  • 支援 Photos Full Access 與 Limited Access。
  • 刪除使用 iOS 系統提供的 destructive confirmation；App 不再重複疊一個自製確認框。
  • iCloud Photos 等 Apple 服務仍由 Apple 與你的系統設定管理。

Gallery

The in-app Gallery displays Apple Photos items you have authorized the app to access.

  • Photos: fit, zoom, double-tap zoom, pan while zoomed, and horizontal paging at fit scale.
  • Videos: play/pause, scrub, and stop playback when leaving the item.
  • Supports Photos Full Access and Limited Access.
  • Deletion uses the destructive confirmation supplied by iOS; the app does not add a second duplicate confirmation dialog.
  • Apple services such as iCloud Photos remain managed by Apple and your system settings.

Profile、Recipe 與外接鏡

你可以使用既有 Profile／Recipe 工作流程保存拍攝設定，並管理外接鏡 Profile。實際可套用的相機能力仍以當下實體鏡頭與裝置 capability 為準。

舊資料若仍保存過去版本使用的 RAW companion 欄位，只作相容性讀取，不會重新啟用已退役的 RAW companion 拍攝行為。

Profiles, Recipes & External Lenses

Use the existing Profile/Recipe workflows to save shooting settings and manage external-lens profiles. Actual camera capability still depends on the current physical lens and device.

If legacy data still contains historical RAW-companion fields, they are compatibility-only and do not reactivate the retired RAW-companion capture behavior.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics help describe app state and problems. They are not a background tracking service.

  • Diagnostic reports are created locally and are not uploaded automatically.
  • They leave the device only after you explicitly generate/export them through an iOS destination you choose.
  • Shipping diagnostics must not contain photo/video content, RAW media content, ROI/metering coordinates, persistent device/account identifiers, location, contacts, payment data, IP/SSID, absolute file paths, or filenames.
  • They may contain app version/build, coarse camera role, settings/state summaries, bounded Focus Assist state, recent metering/RAW persistence outcome summaries, thermal state, and a random per-report ID.
When reporting an issue, prefer the app's official de-identified diagnostic export instead of sharing full system logs, photos, or other material that may contain personal information.

為什麼我看不到 RAW 或 ProRAW？

先確認 Pro+ 權益，再確認目前 iPhone、實體鏡頭與相機 session 是否支援該 RAW family。功能不以機型名稱硬開。

Troubleshooting

Why don't I see RAW or ProRAW?

Check the Pro+ entitlement first, then the current iPhone, physical lens, and camera-session capability for that RAW family. Modes are not enabled by hard-coded model name.

Why is there no JPEG/HEIF next to my RAW file?

This is expected. The current RAW workflow is DNG-only; the quick companion has been retired.

Why does an AE marker appear after a long press?

Long press is the independent exposure-metering action. A normal tap still focuses; the two are intentionally separate.

Why is a feature unavailable even after purchasing Pro/Pro+?

Paid entitlement and hardware capability are separate. A capability the active device/lens does not support is not faked into availability by a purchase.

Why won't the online guide load?

The complete online guide requires a network connection. If your app version includes a local fallback, it can show the last bundled guide while offline.
